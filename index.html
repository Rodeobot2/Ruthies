Our Story

Something New on Plank Rd

Ruthie's Wine & Spirits is under new ownership — and we couldn't be more excited to serve you. We've taken the reins of a beloved community shop and we're bringing fresh energy, a sharper selection, and a whole lot of passion to every bottle on our shelves.

We proudly serve Penfield, Webster, Irondequoit, and the surrounding East Side of Rochester as your go-to neighborhood destination for exceptional wines, spirits, and fine liquors. From everyday favorites to rare finds, our shelves are stocked with thousands of carefully curated selections — and we're constantly adding more.

We believe quality and value should never be a compromise. Whether you're grabbing something for a quiet evening at home or hunting for the perfect gift, our team is here to help you find exactly what you're looking for. Come browse our aisles and see what's new — we'd love to meet you.
